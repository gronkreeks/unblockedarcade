What you'll learn

Builds strategic planning, economic thinking and diplomacy. Balance troop growth against expansion, invest gold in the right buildings, and choose your allies carefully. Meet leaders from world history and learn what each of them valued.

How to play
  • Pick a spawn by clicking land at the start. Every player is protected for the first 30 seconds.
  • Click land next to you to attack it. The attack ratio sets how many of your troops go. Click land across the water to send a boat.
  • Your population regrows fastest at about half your cap. Sitting at the cap wastes growth, and sending everything leaves you open.
  • Troops or civilians? The second slider splits your population. Civilians earn gold but don't fight, so more civilians means a richer nation that's easier to invade.
  • Spend gold on buildings: Cities raise your population cap, Ports earn trade gold, Factories produce gold every second, Defense Posts make nearby land 5× harder to take, and Silos and SAMs are for nukes.
  • Every leader has a playstyle: an always-on bonus and a power you trigger with the ⚡ button or F (raiders plunder gold, island leaders get fast boats, Leonidas has fewer but far stronger troops...). Pick yours with Your leader, and choose who the computer plays with Choose leaders.
  • Every nation has a leader from history with their own personality. Click a leader's portrait on the map or in the leaderboard (or right-click their land) to open diplomacy: see how they feel about you and why, ally with them, trade gold and troops, and ask them to join a war. Gandhi is peaceful... until he isn't.
  • Right-click (or long-press) any nation to ally, break an alliance, or send troops. Breaking an alliance marks you as a traitor.
  • On Earth and Europe, nations start at their real capitals.
  • Warships patrol near their port and sink enemy boats. Click one, then click water, to send it somewhere else.
  • Win by owning 100% of the land. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and press P to pause.
