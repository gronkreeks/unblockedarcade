How to play
  • Pick a spawn by clicking land at the start. Every player is protected for the first 30 seconds.
  • Click land next to you to attack it. The attack ratio sets how many of your troops go. Click land across the water to send a boat.
  • Troops regrow fastest at about half your cap. Sitting at the cap wastes growth, and sending everything leaves you open.
  • Spend gold on buildings: Cities raise your troop cap, Ports earn trade gold, Defense Posts make nearby land 5× harder to take, and Silos and SAMs are for nukes.
  • Right-click (or long-press) any nation to ally, break an alliance, or send troops. Breaking an alliance marks you as a traitor.
  • Win by owning 80% of the land. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and press P to pause.
