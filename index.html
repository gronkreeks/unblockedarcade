Motor Skills & Coordination

Fast, physical games that train hand-eye coordination, timing and reaction speed. Most are two players on one keyboard, so they’re social too.

6 games
Recommended

Volley

Timing & Positioning

Builds timing, spatial positioning and anticipation: read the ball’s arc, guess your opponent’s next move and decide quickly under pressure.

  • 1–2 players · Online

Boxing

Motor Skills

Trains motor skills, hand-eye coordination and reaction speed through split-second key combos.

  • 2 players

Laser Tag

Focus & Precision

Sharpens precision, patience and hand-eye coordination. Pick your moment, aim true and outthink your opponent.

  • 2 players

Tag

Agility & Awareness

Builds spatial awareness, agility and quick tactical decisions while chasing or evading an opponent.

  • 2 players

Pong

Timing & Reflexes

Builds timing, reaction speed and prediction. Read the ball’s angle and move to where it will be, not where it is.

  • 2 players

Star Clicker

Motor Skills & Number Sense

Builds fine motor control and number sense. Click fast and accurately, then compare upgrade costs to grow your stars exponentially.

  • 1 player
  • Phone friendly

EduArcade Chatroom 13+

Talk games, strategies and homework with other players in public rooms, or make a private room for your friends.

Why the chatroom is educational

  • Digital citizenshipPractise the online habits school teaches: be kind, disagree respectfully and think before you post.
  • Staying safe onlineUse a nickname, keep personal details private, and learn when to mute, report and tell a trusted adult.
  • Clear, concise writingThe 200-character limit rewards getting your point across in a few well-chosen words.
  • Teamwork and explainingSwap tactics, explain your thinking and help other players with games and homework.

Use a nickname, not your real name.

Chat rules
  • Be kind. No bullying, hate or sexual talk.
  • Never share personal info: your real name, school, address, phone number or social accounts.
  • No links. They're removed automatically.
  • If anyone makes you uncomfortable, mute or report them and tell a trusted adult.
  • Messages aren't saved. People who get reported by several others are removed.
