Why is the chatroom educational?
  • Digital citizenship. Practise being kind, disagreeing respectfully and thinking before you post.
  • Staying safe online. Use a nickname, keep personal details private, and know when to mute, report and tell an adult.
  • Clear writing. The 200-character limit rewards getting your point across in a few good words.
  • Teamwork. Swap tactics, explain your thinking and help others with games and homework.
How are messages protected?
  • Encrypted. Each message is locked with a key only the people in the room have. The server can’t read it.
  • Filtered. The safety filter runs on every screen, so personal details and slurs stay hidden.
  • Not saved. Messages disappear when a room empties. Reporting shares only that one message so it can be checked.

Use a nickname, not your real name.

Chat rules
  • Be kind. No bullying, hate or sexual talk.
  • Never share personal info: your real name, school, address, phone number or social accounts.
  • No links. They're removed automatically.
  • If anyone makes you uncomfortable, mute or report them and tell a trusted adult.
  • Messages aren't saved. People who get reported by several others are removed.
