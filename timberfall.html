How it works

Pick where to land, grab guns, and swing your axe at trees for wood. When someone opens fire, press Q to drop a barricade between you. The zone keeps shrinking, so keep moving. Last team standing wins.

What you'll learn

Decisions under pressure: manage resources, read the zone, and choose when to fight, build or move.
