THE RULES

  • Each player has a health bar. When the ball lands on your side, you take damage. Empty bar = K.O.
  • Damage grows with the ball's heat level (LV 0 to 5), your combo, pinches, aces and supers.
  • You get 3 touches per side. A 4th touch is a fault. Blocks don't count as a touch.
  • Walls are in play. The ball bounces off them, and so do you.

FIGHTING-GAME TECH

  • Perfect timing: touching the ball in the first 2 active frames of a move gives a PERFECT: a cleaner set, more meter, and a free hot catch.
  • Combos: Set → Spike chains count as hits. Each touch in your chain adds damage. A COUNTER drive (perfectly timed against a hot ball) speeds it up even more.
  • Hitstun: a hot ball that hits your body knocks you back and stuns you. If it then lands before you recover, that's a TRUE COMBO.
  • Wall pinch: stunned players knocked into their back wall get WALL SPLATTED. The ball rebounds off them over and over (PINCH) for chip damage. Press jump right as you hit the wall to WALL TECH out. If you mash jump during hitstun, you lose the tech.
  • Mix-ups: spikes beat defense, blocks beat spikes near the net, tips beat blocks, and catching resets a hot rally.
