Elements

What you'll learnHow the periodic table is organised: atomic numbers go up one by one, periods are rows and groups are columns, and elements in the same group behave alike. You'll learn the chemical symbols (including the ones from Latin, like Fe for iron and Au for gold) and the families: alkali metals, halogens, noble gases and more.
