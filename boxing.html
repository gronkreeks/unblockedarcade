THE RULES

  • Empty your rival's health bar for a K.O. If the timer runs out, whoever has more health wins the round.
  • Hold back (away from your opponent) to block. Blocked kicks still chip a little health.
  • High attacks miss crouching players. Low attacks must be blocked crouching. Jumping attacks must be blocked standing.
  • The ropes are in play. Knock a stunned rival into them and they bounce back for more.

FIGHTING-GAME TECH

  • Combos: punches cancel into kicks when they connect, and kicks cancel into the super. Each extra hit in a combo does a little less damage.
  • Counter hits: hitting someone while their attack is starting up does 25% more damage and stuns longer.
  • Perfect block: start holding back just before a hit lands. No chip damage, extra meter, and you recover faster.
  • Mix-ups: high beats people standing still, low beats people blocking standing, throws beat blocking, and attacks beat throws.
  • Juggles: head kicks, rope bounces and the super send players into the air, where you can hit them up to three times.
