NUMBER RUSH

Mental arithmetic, against the clock. The higher your rank, the harder the sums.

Ranked

60-second races against a rank bot: answer more questions correctly than it does to win. Every rank brings new kinds of question, and within each race the questions get harder the more you get right.

Practice

Pick a topic and take your time: 10 questions, no clock. Stuck? “Show me how” walks through a method step by step, and every mistake is explained.

What you'll learnFast, accurate mental arithmetic: number bonds, times tables, place value, negative numbers, order of operations, squares and roots, percentages, fractions of amounts, powers and decimals. Each answer comes with a method you can reuse, like making a ten, splitting by place value or using 10% to find other percentages.
