How it works

Terrorists win by planting the bomb at site A or B and keeping it from being defused for 40 seconds, or by eliminating the other team. Counter-Terrorists win by eliminating the Ts, defusing the bomb, or running out the clock.

Kills, round wins and even losses earn money. Spend it in the buy menu on guns, armour, a defuse kit and grenades: flashbangs blind, smokes block sight, molotovs block a path with fire.

What you'll learn

Teamwork and money sense: decide when to save and when to spend, read the radar and your teammates' callouts, and plan where to attack or defend.
